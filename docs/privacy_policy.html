Random Decision — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 16, 2026

Data Storage

Random Decision stores your choice lists, shop unlocks and settings locally on your device. We do not collect or upload your personal data.

Internet Usage

The internet connection is used only for:

  • Retrieving IAP configuration from https://api2.blwsmartware.net/R211.json
  • Processing purchases through Google Play Billing

Payments

All in-app purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not store payment card information.
